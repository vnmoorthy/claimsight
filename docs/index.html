How it works

One loop, from evidence to ledger entry.

Most assistants stop at an answer. ClaimSight reaches the last layer of the event's thesis: a transaction with a receipt.

1
Data

Video + order

A 10-second clip. Orders and policy are structured JSON on EdgeOne Makers. No vector database.

2
Intelligence

See the damage

The clip becomes captions and frames; its frames are searched against every prior claim for a twin.

3
Experience

Decision Card

Streaming chat shows the reasoning, the cited clauses, the evidence line and the transaction id.

4
Execution

Policy, twice

The agent applies it; the refund service enforces it again. Over the limit or suspicious? A human in Slack.

5
Transaction

Refund issued

Ledger entry, decision record, a trace in AgentX, and a Blender-rendered Damage Twin as the receipt.

What it does

Built like a teammate, shipped like software.

Reads the evidence

Captions, frames and a summary become the claim's evidence record: "white ceramic mug, chip on the rim at 0:03."

Policy enforced twice

Clauses P1–P6 are cited by the agent and re-checked by the refund service. The model cannot exceed policy even when asked.

Executes the transaction

Refund or replacement, written to the ledger with a transaction id. Above $75 or on a fraud signal, a human decides.

Catches the fraud twin

Before paying, a frame from the new clip is matched against every prior claim. Same footage from another account freezes the claim.

Damage Twin receipt

Blender renders a 3D twin with the damage marked where the video showed it, composited with the customer's frame and the decision.

Proven, not promised

31 golden claims, a policy judge, a deploy gate that fails on regression, PII and secrets monitors, one trace per claim, and a synthetic-video lab.

Damage Twin

The receipt you can orbit.

Every decided claim gets a 6-second Blender render: a procedural twin of the product, a pulsing marker where the damage was described, the customer's frame and the decision as a HUD. It plays inside the Decision Card and the manager's drawer, and it rides along with the escalation.
